📖 What Is Nepal_Quake_Watch?

Nepal_Quake_Watch is a comprehensive, user-friendly application that brings together high-quality seismic data and essential safety information into one easy-to-use platform. Whether you live in Nepal, have family there, or simply care about disaster awareness, this tool keeps you informed with real-time updates and critical preparedness resources.

💡 Simply put: Nepal_Quake_Watch tells you where earthquakes are happening right now, how strong they are, how many people might be affected, and where to get help if needed. All in one glance.

✨ Key Features

📡 Live Earthquake Data
Combines official USGS and NEMRC catalogues for the most complete and accurate picture of seismic activity across Nepal.
🗺️ ShakeMap Intensity
Visual maps show you exactly how strongly the ground is shaking in different areas, helping you gauge the impact immediately.
👥 Population Exposure
Uses official census data to show how many people live in affected wards, giving you a clear picture of potential impact.
🏚️ Building Vulnerability
Identifies which structures may be at higher risk during seismic events, promoting safer decision-making.
🏥 Emergency Resources
Quick access to hospitals and official emergency numbers, so you always know where to turn when seconds matter.
📱 Interactive Maps
Powered by Leaflet technology, easy-to-navigate maps require no technical expertise. Just click and explore.
